Guide Overview: How Validation Marks Are Calculated
Theoretical Evaluation Framework: Designed for project guides to evaluate startup feasibility by comparing parameters across 4 foundational research sources.
1. Theoretical Basis for Feasibility Scoring:
  • Market Customer Demand (30% Weight): Evaluates addressable market size, growth CAGR %, and target customer problem urgency based on industry market research.
  • Innovation Uniqueness (25% Weight): Assesses IP originality, technological differentiation, and competitive edge over existing industry alternatives.
  • Technical Feasibility (25% Weight): Evaluates implementation complexity, stack readiness, and developer skill requirements.
  • Business Profitability (20% Weight): Evaluates financial ROI projections, cost structure sustainability, and break-even timeline.
2. Startup Overall Feasibility Mark Calculation:
Overall Validation Mark = Innovation (25%) + Market Demand (30%) + Technical Feasibility (25%) + Business Profitability (20%)

3. Dashboard Average Validation Score Metric:

The Average Validation Score displayed on the dashboard stat card is calculated by averaging overall feasibility marks across all saved startup projects:

Average Validation Score = (Sum of Overall Scores of All Saved Projects) ÷ (Total Saved Projects Count)
Note for Guides: If no saved projects exist yet, the system displays the baseline feasibility benchmark of 86.5/100 until startup concepts are generated.
